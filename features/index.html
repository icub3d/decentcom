How decentcom works.

A communication platform built on the principles of identity ownership and community sovereignty.

Identity is a Key, Not an Account

In most chat services, your account exists as a row in the platform's database. In decentcom, your account is an Ed25519 cryptographic key pair that *you* generate.

  • ✓ No email, phone number, or personal info required.
  • ✓ Use a 24-word recovery phrase to restore your identity anywhere.
  • ✓ Your identity is portable across all servers you join.
🔑

Privacy by Design

Authentication is done via challenge-response signing. Servers verify who you are without ever seeing a password or storing a secret.

  • ✓ Private keys never leave your client's core process.
  • ✓ No central operator logging your connections.
  • ✓ You choose which servers to trust with your metadata.
🛡️

Community Sovereignty

decentcom is designed for groups that want total control over their data and community.

  • ✓ Self-host with a single binary or Docker container.
  • ✓ Multiple membership modes: Open, Invite-only, or Allowlist.
  • ✓ Full role-based permission system for granular control.
🏘️
